### Results Table

This table displays the processed data along with the analysis performed by the `stancer` model. You can use this view to inspect individual results and compare them with your source text.

#### Features:
*   **Search and Filter**: Use the search bar to find specific keywords or phrases within your corpus.
*   **Pagination**: Use the navigation at the bottom to browse through the results if you have analysed multiple rows.
*   **Dynamic Columns**:
    *   **Source Text**: The original content provided for analysis.
    *   **Target**: The entity or claim being evaluated.
    *   **Stance**: The final output from the model (Categorical, Likert, or Numeric).
    *   **True Label**: If provided during import, your "Ground Truth" values will be shown alongside the model's predictions for easy comparison.

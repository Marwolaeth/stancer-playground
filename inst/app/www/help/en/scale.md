# Scale

Stance Scale:
1.  **Categorical**: Simple classification (Negative, Neutral, Positive). Most stable.
2.  **Likert**: A 5-point scale from "Strongly Disagree" to "Strongly Agree". Recommended for nuanced analysis.
3.  **Numeric**: A score ranging from -1 to 1.
    
    *   ⚠️ **Note:** The numeric scale may be unstable in the current version. We recommend using Categorical or Likert scales for more reliable results.
